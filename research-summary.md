# Detailed Cybercrime Research

## Introduction
Cybercrime refers to any criminal act made with the use of a computer, network, or the internet. As technology advances, cybercriminals 
continue to improve new methods to exploit vulnerabilities and steal sensitive information.

## Types of Cybercrime 

### 1.Phishing 
Phishing involves sending fake emails or messages that seem legit in an attempt to trick victims into revealing sensitive

### 2.Malware 
Malware is malicious software made to damage systems, steal data, or gain unauthorized information.

### 3.Ransomware 
Ransomware encrypts files and commands payment from victims for access restoration.

### 4.Identity Theft
Cybercriminals can steal personal information and pose as the victim to commit fraud and other acts.

### 5.Unauthorised Access
Hackers abuse security weaknesses to access systems, network, and information.

## Impact of Cybercrime 

Financial losses
Data Breach
Loss of privacy 
Reputation decline
Disruption of business operations

## Cybercrime Best Practices

Use strong passwords
Enable Multi factor Authentication 
Regular update software
Install antivirus software
Perform regular backups
Educate users on cyber threat

## Conclusion
Cybercrime remains one of the biggest threats in the digital age. Organisations and individuals must adopt strong cybersecurity practices to defend their systems and data from cyberattacks.
